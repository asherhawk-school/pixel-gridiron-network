# Pixel Gridiron Network

A 24/7 pixel-art college football TV channel that runs in the browser.

**Watch:** https://asherhawk-school.github.io/pixel-gridiron-network/

- **74 schools in 5 conferences**, parodies inspired by real programs (every player, coach and sponsor is made up).
- **Full 85-man rosters** with positions, classes, redshirts, heights, weights, hometowns, recruiting stars, overall ratings and live season stats.
- **Recruiting:** each season a national class of about 1,800 prospects commits over the season, with flips and a signing day. Class rankings update live.
- **Transfer portal:** opens with bowl season. Backups, buried four-stars and stars on losing teams enter, and most pick new schools before next season.
- **Season cycle:** 12 regular-season weeks, conference title games, bowls, a 12-team playoff, signing day, then the offseason. Players develop, graduate, declare for the draft or redshirt, and program prestige moves with results.
- **Clock-synced:** everything is derived from the time, so everyone watching sees the same snap and the same commitments. A league week airs every 6 hours; a full season takes about 3.75 days.
- Keys: **G** program guide, **M** sound, **F** fullscreen. The league desk under the TV has standings, the Top 25, team pages, the recruiting board and the portal.

Files: `index.html` (page), `league.js` (league engine and game sim), `tv.js` (broadcast), `pages.js` (league desk). No build step. To jump to a moment for testing, add `?t=2026-11-15T14:10:00Z` to the URL.
