Schools here are parodies inspired by real programs; every player, coach and sponsor is made up. Games, recruiting and the transfer portal all run off the clock, so everyone watching sees the same snap and the same commitments at the same moment. A league week airs every six hours, and a full season with conference title games, bowls, a 12-team playoff, signing day and the portal runs about three and three-quarter days.
